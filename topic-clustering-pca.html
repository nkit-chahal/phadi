📉 PCA (Principal Component Analysis)

Reduce dimensionality while preserving maximum variance. "Squashing data onto the most informative axes".

Steps for PCA

  1. Standardize the data (Mean=0, Std=1).
  2. Compute Covariance Matrix.
  3. Compute Eigenvectors and Eigenvalues.
  4. Sort Eigenvalues (highest variance first).
  5. Project data onto top k Eigenvectors.

The "Curse of Dimensionality"

As dimensions increase, data becomes sparse. Distance metrics (Euclidean) lose meaning. Models overfit easily. PCA fixes this by removing noise dimensions.

🎤 Interview Questions

Q1 How do you choose K in K-Means?

Elbow Method: Plot inertia (sum of squared distances) vs K. Look for the "elbow" where improvements flatten out. Silhouette Score: Measures how similar object is to its own cluster vs other clusters (-1 to 1).

Q2 PCA vs t-SNE?

PCA: Linear, deterministic, preserves global structure. Good for preprocessing. t-SNE: Non-linear, probabilistic, preserves local structure. Good for visualization ONLY (clusters in t-SNE don't always mean real clusters).
