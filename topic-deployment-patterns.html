Deployment Patterns

Safe strategies to roll out ML models to production

1. Blue/Green Deployment

Mechanism: Maintain two identical environments (Blue = Current, Green = New). Router switches 100% traffic instantly.

  • Pros: Instant rollback (millisecond switch), clean cutover.
  • Cons: Expensive (Double infrastructure cost). Warning: Database schema compatibility issues.
  • Use Case: Critical services where downtime is unacceptable.

2. Canary Deployment

Mechanism: Roll out new model to a small subset of users (e.g., 5%) first. Monitor metrics. Gradually increase to 100%.

  • Pros: Limits blast radius of bugs. Low risk.
  • Cons: Slower rollout time. Need complex routing logic (sticky sessions).
  • Use Case: Standard model updates where you want to verify performance on real traffic.

3. Shadow Deployment

Mechanism: Incoming request is sent to BOTH existing and new model. Only existing model's response is returned to user. New model's response is logged for analysis.

  • Pros: ZERO risk to user. Best for testing accuracy/latency on real data.
  • Cons: Double compute cost. No feedback on user reaction (CTR, conversions).
  • Use Case: Major architecture changes (e.g., migrating from XGBoost to Deep Learning).

4. A/B Testing

Mechanism: Split traffic 50/50 (or similar) into "Control" (Old) and "Variant" (New). Analyze statistical significance of business metrics (Conversion, Turn-rate).

  • Pros: Definitive proof of business value.
  • Cons: Risk of losing revenue on the bad variant. Long duration to reach significance.
  • Use Case: Optimizing business logic, UI changes, Recommendation algorithms.
